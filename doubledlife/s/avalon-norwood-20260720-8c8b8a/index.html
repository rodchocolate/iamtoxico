Avalon Norwood is a residential community in Norwood, Massachusetts, offering one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments and townhomes for lease. It is managed by AvalonBay Communities.

residential offerings

The community provides a variety of home types, including lofts and townhouses, with living spaces featuring modern kitchens equipped with stainless steel appliances and stone countertops. Residents can choose between unfurnished units and 'Furnished+' options for turnkey living. Technology is integrated through the AvalonConnect package, which provides high-speed internet and digital resident services such as online rent payment and service requests.

community amenities

Avalon Norwood features a state-of-the-art fitness center with a flex studio, virtual fitness, and Peloton’s bike for live and on-demand studio cycling classes. Other amenities include a pool, a resident lounge with an entertaining kitchen, and the WAG dog run for pet-friendly living. The community also offers a variety of programs such as the Bilt Rewards Alliance, a Relocation Advantage program for transition assistance, and an affordable housing program for qualifying residents.

location and accessibility

The community is located at 155 Lenox St, Norwood, MA 02062. It is situated 0.1 miles from the Norwood Central Commuter Rail and is conveniently positioned near the I-95 and 128 corridor and I-93. For shopping, dining, and entertainment, Legacy Place is located 4 miles away, and the community is 30 minutes from Boston.
