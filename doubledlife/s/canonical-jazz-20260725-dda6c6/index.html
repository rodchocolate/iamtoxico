This research note outlines the "Canonical jazz" workstream, a one-shot acquisition pipeline designed to collect essential 20th-century jazz recordings. Unlike discovery-driven methods, this project targets a predefined list of essential albums to validate the Rekordbox-USB "north star."

Definition of the Jazz Canon

In this context, the jazz canon is defined as a well-known and public list of essential albums and standards. It is treated as a "canonical" rather than "exploratory" dataset, meaning the pipeline bypasses discovery and synthesis in favor of a direct process: reading a known list, looking up tracks, downloading, tagging, and filing.

Approach to Defining the Canon

The canon is constructed via a "Canonical seed builder" that scrapes and aggregates data from the following sources:

  • Wikipedia: "List of jazz albums considered the greatest ever" and "List of jazz standards."
  • Penguin Guide to Jazz: "Crown" recipients.
  • AllMusic: "Jazz Top 1000" editorial picks.
  • Discogs: Jazz releases from 1917-2000 with a rating of 4.0 stars or higher.
  • MusicBrainz: Release-groups tagged as jazz and filtered for canonical figures.

To categorize these entries, a `consensus_score` is used to track how many independent sources mention a specific work. A score of 3+ designates "core canon," while a score of 1-2 designates "broader significant" works.

Canonical Artists and Eras

The project centers on 20th-century jazz, specifically covering the era from 1917 to 2000. The "Significant canon" target includes:

  • Every headliner from the Blue Note, Impulse, and Prestige labels.
  • European labels, including SteepleChase, Black Lion, and Enja.
  • Significant sideman dates.
